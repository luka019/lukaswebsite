# DLG — specialist law firm design study

Reviewed public homepages and service architecture on 7 November 2026:
- https://www.bristows.com/ — topic-led editorial hero, distinctive visual stories, clear technology specialism.
- https://kempitlaw.com/ — precise boutique positioning, serif-led hierarchy, strong negative space and restrained geometry.
- https://www.twobirds.com/en — business-change positioning, connected capabilities, people and practical insights.

These are relevant specialist references, not a claim of a universal design ranking. Bristows and Kemp were also inspected in a rendered browser. No layouts, slogans, imagery, client claims or testimonials were copied.

DLG interpretation: a numbered legal folio replaces the repetitive service-card catalogue. Native details disclosures retain keyboard and no-JavaScript support, with a direct service link in every expanded section. The first service is open initially. Responsive line lengths, original vector illustrations and a single understated expansion motion make the catalogue feel deliberately composed. The approved four-topic hero, cool blue identity, founder credentials, bilingual content and business pathways remain.
