# DLG — Perspective visual direction

User rejected the legal folio and requested a complete new design. The latest direction restores directly visible service cards and introduces an architectural photographic hero on navy, a contrasting white masthead, generous typography, coordinated pale blue sections and a two-column service composition. No paper sculpture or accordion is used in the homepage hero or services.

Research references: https://www.bristows.com/ ; https://kempitlaw.com/ ; https://www.twobirds.com/en ; https://www.cooley.com/ ; https://www.mofo.com/ . The design applies a clear lead message, strong image/typography relationship and navigable expertise, without copying layouts, slogans, endorsements or imagery. The existing local architecture photograph is illustrative and does not represent a DLG office.

Preserved: approved legal content, actual founder credentials, Georgian and English routes, direct contact links and accessible motion controls. Browser QA checks the new intentional white/navy surface contrast, photographic asset, service count, language navigation and nine viewport widths.
